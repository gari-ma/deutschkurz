ABOUT THIS TEXTBOOK
-------------------
This textbook is designed for learners at the A2 level of
the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). Each
chapter contains a long German reading passage, a full
English translation, vocabulary tables, comprehension
exercises, writing tasks, and speaking practice.

Work through one chapter at a time. Do not rush. Read each
passage at least twice — once for overall meaning, once
carefully for language detail.

HOW TO USE THIS TEXTBOOK
=============================================================

Each chapter follows this structure:

  1. Learning Objectives
     What you will be able to do after this chapter.

  2. Pre-Reading Vocabulary
     Key words to know before reading the passage.

  3. German Reading Passage (500–800 words)
     A realistic, engaging story set in Germany.

  4. English Translation
     Paragraph-by-paragraph translation.

  5. Vocabulary Table
     All important new words with articles, plurals,
     meanings, and example sentences.

  6. Reading Comprehension Exercises
     Multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank,
     and short answer questions.

  7. Writing Exercise
     A guided writing task with a model answer.

  8. Speaking Practice
     Dialogues and speaking prompts.

  9. Chapter Summary and Checklist
     What you should know before moving on.

 10. Answer Key
     Answers to all exercises with brief explanations.
